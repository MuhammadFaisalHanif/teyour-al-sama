Frequently Asked Questions

إجابات واضحة على كل استفسارات صيانة وضمان السيارات

How do you calculate repair estimates? Will the final bill change?

No unexpected charges ever. We provide a transparent, itemized digital quotation detailing both labor and spare parts costs prior to starting any repair. If our technicians identify an unforeseen secondary issue during disassembly, we halt work and obtain your explicit WhatsApp approval first.

Do you provide a warranty on parts and labor?

Yes. All mechanical and electrical repairs include an official workshop labor guarantee, alongside standard manufacturer warranties (up to 12 months) on all installed genuine OEM replacement parts.

How fast can I get my car back?

Standard periodic oil changes, brake pad installations, and basic AC top-ups are completed within 2 to 3 hours. For complex diagnostic troubleshooting or major accident rebuilds, we deliver an upfront timeline with daily WhatsApp photo progress reports.

Do you use original factory parts or aftermarket alternatives?

We prioritize 100% genuine OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) components sourced through verified UAE distributors. Upon request, we can also supply certified Tier-1 aftermarket brands (such as Bosch, Brembo, or Denso) with distinct price comparisons.
